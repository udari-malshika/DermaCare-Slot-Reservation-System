CARBON LASER
PEEL TREATMENT

Get closer to your skin and hair goals with clinical treatments tailored exactly to your unique profile.

More Details about Carbon Laser Peel Treatment:

A layer of liquid carbon lotion is applied across the face to absorb deep-seated oil, dead skin, and toxins within the pores. A Q-Switched laser then passes over the area, vaporizing the carbon particles along with all absorbed impurities while stimulating mild dermal collagen production.

Primary Indications: Excessive sebum production, active acne breakouts, enlarged pores, uneven skin tone, and general skin dullness.

DermoCare Configuration:

  • Slot Duration: 30 to 45 minutes.
  • Buffer Window: 10 minutes.
  • Pre-Procedure Lock: Zero downtime procedure; requires pre-treatment confirmation of no active severe eczema or open facial wounds.
