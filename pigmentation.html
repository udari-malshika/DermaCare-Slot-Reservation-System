Get closer to your skin and hair goals with clinical treatments tailored exactly to your unique profile.

More Details about Pigmentation Peel Treatment:

Medical-grade chemical peels combine exfoliating acids with brightening ingredients to reduce excess melanin, improve uneven tone, and reveal smoother-looking skin.

Primary Indications: Melasma, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, sun spots, acne marks, and uneven facial discoloration.

DermoCare Configuration:

  • Slot Duration: 30 minutes.
  • Buffer Window: 5 minutes.
  • Pre-Procedure Lock: System locks until the patient confirms non-use of prescription retinoids or tretinoin for 5 days before treatment.
