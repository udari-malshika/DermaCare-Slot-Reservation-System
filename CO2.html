More Details about CO2 Laser Treatment:

Operates at a 10,600 nm wavelength that targets water in skin cells. The laser light energy rapidly vaporizes the damaged outer epidermal layer while delivering deep thermal heat into the dermis. This contracts existing fibers and triggers a long-term collagen remodeling process.

Primary Indications: Deep static wrinkles, severe acne scars, surgical scars, actinic keratoses (precancerous spots), uneven texture, and benign skin lesions (warts/skin tags).

DermoCare Configuration:

  • Slot Duration: 60 to 120 minutes (varies by targeted area size).
  • Buffer Window: 15 minutes.
  • Pre-Procedure Lock: Requires patient acknowledgment of a 7-14 day healing downtime and avoidance of direct sun exposure prior to booking.
